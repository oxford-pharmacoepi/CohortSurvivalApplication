# Study overview

This application presents aggregate results from the CohortSurvival example
study of recorded beta-blocker exposure after myocardial infarction (MI).

MI events form the target cohort. The outcome is the first beta-blocker
exposure recorded after MI; previous beta-blocker exposure does not exclude a
person. Death is treated as a competing event in the competing-risk analysis.
Follow-up is limited to the first 365 days after MI.

The study is descriptive. It does not estimate treatment effectiveness,
dispensing, or adherence, and its results should not be interpreted causally.

Use the **Analysis type** selector in the **CohortSurvival** panel to switch
between single-event and competing-risk results. Only one analysis type is
shown at a time so their summaries, events, attrition, and plots remain clearly
separated.
